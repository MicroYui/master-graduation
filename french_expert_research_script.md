# Research Introduction Script

## English

UAVs, mobile robots, edge cameras and vehicular nodes are increasingly used to provide field services through temporary multi-hop networks. In these scenarios, communication is no longer just a background channel. Sensor data need to be forwarded continuously, local observations need to be exchanged among agents, and dispersed evidence needs to be fused before a reliable decision can be made.

The difficulty is that service quality is affected by several constraints at the same time. A routing decision may improve short-term throughput, but it may also overuse a key relay node and shorten the service lifetime. Similarly, sending more semantic evidence may improve collaborative reasoning, but it may also consume more tokens, miss the available communication window, or be lost over an unreliable link.

Based on this observation, my research studies QoS-aware communication along a connected line. LEADR focuses on sustainable service data transfer in energy-limited FANETs through joint routing and power control. EviComRL moves to the semantic layer and studies which evidence should be exchanged for multi-agent visual reasoning under limited and unreliable communication. The robustness analysis then connects the two layers by evaluating whether semantic communication policies remain effective when delivery probability, latency and energy cost are generated by physical link and energy dynamics.

## 中文

无人机、移动机器人、边缘摄像头和车联网节点正在越来越多地通过临时多跳网络提供现场服务。在这类场景中，通信不再只是一个底层通道。传感数据需要持续回传，局部观测需要在多个智能体之间交换，分散的证据也需要被融合之后才能形成可靠决策。

难点在于，服务质量会同时受到多种约束影响。一个路由决策可能提高短期吞吐量，但也可能过度使用某个关键中继节点，从而缩短服务持续时间。同样，发送更多语义证据可能提升协同推理质量，但也会带来更高 token 成本，可能错过通信窗口，或者在不可靠链路上丢失。

基于这一观察，我的研究围绕服务质量感知的协同通信形成一条衔接的主线。LEADR 关注能量受限飞行自组网中的可持续服务数据传输，通过路由和功率联合控制延长服务周期。EviComRL 进一步转向语义层，研究在有限且不可靠通信条件下，应该为多智能体视觉推理交换哪些证据。鲁棒性分析则把这两层连接起来，评估当送达概率、时延和能耗由物理链路与能量动态驱动时，语义通信策略是否仍然有效。
